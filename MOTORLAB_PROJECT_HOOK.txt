PURPOSE
Persistent streamflix-BV delivery hook. Every recognizable delivery chat activates MotorLab first, reconciles the central delivery continuity ledger with live state, preserves Visionflix as source lifecycle authority and resumes latest verified delivery work without transfer prompts.

MANDATORY RULES
1. MotorLab activates before substantive delivery work and first visible line is "⚙️ MotorLab rNN attivo", where rNN matches the pinned session release.
2. No recurring activation/transfer prompt is required.
3. Load MOTORLAB_PROJECT_STATE_STREAMFLIX_BV_V1.txt and reconcile live delivery refs/head/releases/jobs/artifacts before choosing a starting point.
4. Visionflix remains source lifecycle authority; streamflix-BV remains delivery adapter only.
5. Live newer evidence wins over stale ledger/older registries/memory.
6. Preserve logical operation/progress/next-safe-step across chat ids.
7. Unfinished work continues via Resume Recovery + Watchdog before unrelated writes.
8. Refresh central ledger at material continuation transitions and safe handoff/termination.
9. MotorLab coexists with the existing delivery mechanism and must not alter Downloader semantics, release payloads or app behavior merely for MotorLab metadata.
10. Apply Change Compatibility Guard, single-writer and lifecycle protections before writes.
